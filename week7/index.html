1Study: Chapter 5 Part B

Topics 3 and 4 of Chapter 5, Probability Distributions and Data Modelling.

Random variables. A discrete variable is counted (customers, orders, defects). A continuous variable is measured (time, weight, money spent).
Expected value E(x). The long-run average: multiply each value by its probability and add them up. Example: 0 sales (0.3), 1 sale (0.5), 2 sales (0.2) → 0×0.3 + 1×0.5 + 2×0.2 = 0.9 sales.
Normal distribution. A bell-shaped curve. The mean sits in the centre, and the curve is symmetric around it.
z-score. How many standard deviations a value is from the mean: z = (value − mean) ÷ SD.
Excel 2019. =NORM.DIST(x, mean, SD, TRUE) gives the probability of x or less. For "more than x", use =1-NORM.DIST(x, mean, SD, TRUE).
